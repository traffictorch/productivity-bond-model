Frequently Asked Questions

Find answers to the most common questions about the Global Vital Signs Dashboard, its data, and features.

🌍 What is the Global Vital Signs Dashboard?▼

The Global Vital Signs Dashboard is a live, open‑source data platform that tracks 14+ key indicators of planetary and societal health – including atmospheric CO₂, global temperature anomalies, GDP, government debt, poverty, and infant mortality. It provides a real‑time snapshot of the world’s climate, economy, and social conditions, sourced directly from public APIs.

📊 What data sources power the dashboard?▼

All data is pulled from reputable public APIs:

  • World Bank – economic indicators (GDP, government debt, poverty rates, etc.)
  • NASA / Global Warming API – atmospheric CO₂ levels and global temperature anomalies
  • Other open data providers – social indicators like infant mortality and literacy rates

All sources are clearly cited within the dashboard itself.

🔄 How often is the data updated?▼

The dashboard updates automatically whenever the underlying APIs refresh their data. Most economic indicators (GDP, debt) are updated quarterly or annually, while climate data (CO₂, temperature) is updated in near‑real‑time. The timestamp of the latest update is displayed on each chart.

🤖 What is the AI Data Interpreter?▼

The AI Data Interpreter is a built‑in tool that analyses the latest global data across all 14+ indicators and generates a concise, plain‑English summary of current trends. With one click, you get an instant snapshot of planetary and societal health – perfect for quick briefings, research, or decision‑making. The AI does not store or share your data; all analysis happens in your browser.

📅 Can I adjust the date ranges on the charts?▼

Yes. Each metric is presented as an interactive chart with adjustable date ranges. You can zoom in on specific periods (e.g., the last 10 years, the post‑pandemic era, or the entire available dataset) to better understand short‑term fluctuations and long‑term trends.

💰 Is this dashboard free to use?▼

Yes, completely free and open source. The dashboard is provided as a public good by Traffic Torch and the PBM Productivity Lab. You can use it for research, policy work, journalism, or personal curiosity – no registration or payment required.

🔗 Can I embed this dashboard on my own website?▼

Absolutely. The dashboard is designed to be easily embedded via a simple iframe. You can find the embed code on the dashboard page itself. It adapts to dark/light mode and works on all screen sizes.

📋 What specific indicators are tracked?▼

The dashboard tracks a curated set of 14+ indicators across three domains:

  • Climate: Atmospheric CO₂ (ppm), Global Temperature Anomaly (°C)
  • Economy: Global GDP, Government Debt (% of GDP), Inflation, Unemployment
  • Social: Poverty Rate, Infant Mortality, Life Expectancy, Literacy Rate, and more

New indicators are added periodically – check the dashboard for the full list.
