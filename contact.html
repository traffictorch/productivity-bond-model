Traffic Torch – Research & Incentive Design is the team behind the Productivity Bond Model. We bring together expertise in economics, finance, systems thinking, and institutional design to address the fundamental contradiction between unlimited financial promises and a finite planet.

We’re actively engaging with policymakers, central banks, multilateral institutions, academics, and citizen groups who share a commitment to building a financial system that rewards productivity, not extraction. If you're exploring sovereign debt reform, developing state‑contingent instruments, or researching the transition to a steady‑state economy, we welcome your perspective.
