📊 What is the National Productivity Index (NPI)? ▼

The National Productivity Index (NPI) is a composite measure of economic efficiency. It calculates how effectively an economy converts its key inputs – Labour, Energy, Materials, and Capital – into economic output (GDP).

NPI = GDP ÷ (Labour0.40 × Energy0.25 × Materials0.20 × Capital0.15)

A higher NPI means the economy produces more value from the same inputs – a sign of genuine progress, not just growth.

🌍 How does the NPI Index Tracker work? ▼

The NPI Index Tracker is an interactive tool that calculates the NPI using real‑world data from 47 countries, spanning 1990 to 2023.

You can:

  • Select any country – from the USA and China to Nigeria and Vietnam.
  • Choose your year range – from 1990 to 2023.
  • Switch between three weight modes – Book NPI (fixed weights), Country Preset (auto‑applied based on economy type), or Custom (fully adjustable).
  • Visualise the NPI trend – with an interactive line chart.
  • See the component breakdown – which factor drives productivity.
  • Get a prescription – auto‑generated diagnosis with actionable recommendations.
  • Export the data – download a CSV of the current chart.
  • Share your setup – copy a shareable link that encodes your country, years, and weights.
  • Ask the AI – get a detailed analysis with country‑specific context.

⚖️ What are the three weight modes? ▼
📘 Book NPI
Fixed weights: 40% Labour · 25% Energy · 20% Materials · 15% Capital. This is the canonical version defined in The Infinite Debt Problem.
🌍 Country Preset
Weights auto‑adjust based on the country's economic category (Tech, Resource, Manufacturing, Developing, Service, Mixed).
⚙️ Custom
Fully adjustable sliders that automatically rebalance to sum to 100% – perfect for exploring alternative assumptions.
📂 Where does the data come from? ▼

All data is sourced from publicly available international databases:

GDP – World Bank
Hours Worked – ILO / OECD
Energy Consumption – IEA
Materials Consumption – UNEP
Capital Stock – OECD / Penn World Table

⚠️ Capital stock data is estimated. This is an empirical proxy for the NPI framework – not an official government statistic.

🤖 What does the AI analysis do? ▼

The AI Analysis feature provides:

  • A structured summary – NPI trend, best and worst performing components, and country‑specific economic context.
  • Actionable advice – tailored recommendations based on the country's economic category (Tech, Resource, Manufacturing, Developing, Service, Mixed).
  • Component growth breakdown – showing the annual growth rate of GDP, Labour, Energy, Materials, and Capital.
  • Clear disclaimer – AI can make mistakes. Always verify with your own research.

The AI is powered by a Cloudflare Worker with rule‑based insights, with optional integration for LLM‑powered analysis.

📥 Can I export or share my results? ▼

Yes! The NPI Index Tracker includes two powerful sharing features:

  • 📥 Export CSV – Download the current chart data (Year, GDP, Hours, Energy, Materials, Capital, NPI) as a CSV file for further analysis in Excel or Google Sheets.
  • 📋 Copy Link – Generate a shareable URL that encodes your country, year range, weight mode, and custom weights. Share it with colleagues, students, or on social media.

🏛️ What is the Productivity Bond Model? ▼

The Productivity Bond Model is a framework for sovereign debt that links returns to measurable productivity gains rather than fixed compounding. It addresses the fundamental contradiction between unlimited financial promises and a finite planet.

Key features:

  • Productivity‑linked returns – investors are rewarded when the economy becomes more efficient.
  • Productivity Dividend – a share of productivity gains flows to citizens.
  • Countercyclical design – payments rise and fall with economic performance.
  • Finite‑planet compatible – rewards doing more with less, not endless expansion.

Monte Carlo simulations (10,000 paths, 10‑year horizon) show a ~3.1% cost reduction vs conventional bonds.
