Compound Curve

See how your debt compounds over time — and how small changes bend the curve.

Enter your numbers and click Project the Future to see the compounding effect.

📖 What do these terms mean?▼
Annual Debt — The yearly financial loss from your productivity or spending leak. This is the amount you want to reduce.
Growth Rate — The annual percentage increase in your debt. Even 10% can double your debt in ~7 years.
Years — The time horizon you want to project into the future. 10 years shows the true cost of inaction.
Reduction — The amount you can cut from your annual leak. Small cuts compound into huge savings over time.

Frequently Asked Questions

What is the Compound Curve tool?▼

The Compound Curve tool projects how your annual debt grows over time due to compound interest. It shows the difference between taking action and doing nothing.

How do I use the Compound Curve?▼

Enter your annual debt, growth rate, time horizon, and the amount you can reduce annually. Click 'Project the Future' to see the chart and savings.

What does the Growth Rate represent?▼

The growth rate is the annual percentage increase in your debt. Even 10% can double your debt in ~7 years.

How much can I save by reducing my annual debt?▼

Small cuts compound into huge savings over time. The tool shows you exactly how much you save in the final year.

Is the Compound Curve tool free?▼

Yes, completely free and open source.
