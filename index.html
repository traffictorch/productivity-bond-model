❓ Frequently Asked Questions

What is the infinite debt problem? ▼
The gap between exponential global debt growth (over $300 trillion) and finite planetary resources. The financial system assumes unlimited growth on a planet with limits.
What is a Productivity Bond? ▼
A sovereign bond with a coupon linked to productivity growth. It offers inflation protection, a 0% real floor, and a 5% cap, sharing risks between government and investors.
How does the NPI work? ▼
The National Productivity Index measures GDP per unit of labour (40%), energy (25%), materials (20%), and capital (15%) using a geometric average to prevent manipulation.
